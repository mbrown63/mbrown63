The History of Anime

Anime history began in Japan in the early 20th century, with some of the earliest known animated films appearing in the 1910s. Before film animation, Japan already had forms of visual storytelling such as magic lantern shows, shadow plays, and illustrated scrolls. In the late 1910s, animators such as Ōten Shimokawa, Jun’ichi Kōuchi, and Seitarō Kitayama helped establish the foundation of Japanese animation. During the 1930s and World War II, animation continued to develop and was sometimes used for educational and propaganda films. After World War II, Japanese animation grew rapidly, especially with the influence of Osamu Tezuka and his studio, Mushi Production. Tezuka’s Astro Boy, which premiered in 1963, became one of the first major anime television series and helped shape the modern anime industry. Over the following decades, anime continued to grow in Japan and eventually became a major part of global popular culture.

Today, anime has become a major form of entertainment around the world, reaching audiences through television, theaters, streaming services, and social media. The internet has made anime easier to access internationally, allowing fans to watch new series shortly after they are released in Japan. Streaming platforms such as Netflix, Crunchyroll, and other services have helped anime reach millions of viewers outside Japan. Popular series such as Demon Slayer, Attack on Titan, Jujutsu Kaisen, My Hero Academia, and One Piece have developed large international fanbases. Anime movies have also achieved major success, with Demon Slayer: Mugen Train becoming one of the highest-grossing films of 2020 and The Boy and the Heron winning the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature in 2024. Anime has also influenced music, fashion, video games, movies, and Western animation. With new technology and global streaming continuing to expand, anime has become more accessible and influential than ever before. Today, anime is no longer viewed as a niche form of Japanese entertainment but as an important part of global popular culture.

Important Moments in Anime History

  • Japanese animation began developing in the early 1900s.
  • Astro Boy premiered on Japanese television in 1963.
  • Anime became increasingly popular around the world during the 1980s and 1990s.
  • During the 2000s, anime gained significant international recognition and success.
  • Anime has brought Japan and other Asian cultures more popularity around the world.
