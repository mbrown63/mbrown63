Fenty Beauty

Fashion Icon

Poor to Rich

Rihanna was born Robyn Rihanna Fenty on February 20, 1988, in Saint Michael, Barbados. As a teenager, she met producer Evan Rogers, which helped launch her music career. She later moved to the United States to record music and signed with Def Jam. Her early songs introduced her Caribbean-influenced sound, while later albums allowed her to experiment with pop, R&B, dance and other styles.

More than just Music

Rihanna expanded her career beyond music into beauty, fashion and philanthropy. She founded the Clara Lionel Foundation in 2012, which supports health and education initiatives around the world. Her business ventures, including Fenty Beauty, have also made her an influential entrepreneur in addition to being an entertainer.
