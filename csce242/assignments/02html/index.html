Disney Pocahontas

What's the truth behind Disney Pocahontas???

All about Otters

Disney’s Pocahontas is very different from the true story of Pocahontas. The real Pocahontas was a Powhatan girl born around 1596 and was also known as Amonute or Matoaka. Unlike the Disney movie, Pocahontas and John Smith did not have a romantic relationship. She was only about 10–12 years old when she met the 27-year-old John Smith. The famous story about Pocahontas saving Smith’s life is also debated because historians are unsure whether the event actually happened the way Smith described it.

Pocahontas’s real life was much more difficult than what is shown in the Disney movie. In 1613, English colonists captured her and held her hostage. During this time, she converted to Christianity and took the name Rebecca. She later married John Rolfe, not John Smith, and they had a son named Thomas. Pocahontas eventually traveled to England, where she became seriously ill and died in 1617 at only about 20 or 21 years old.
