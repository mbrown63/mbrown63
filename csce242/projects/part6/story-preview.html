HOW IT ALL BEGAN

UNISOGHN started with four individuals who each came
from different backgrounds, experiences, and sounds,
but were connected by the same thing — a love for music.
What began as late night studio sessions and shared ideas
quickly turned into something bigger than ourselves.

Through every challenge, every late night, and every
moment of growth, we found a bond built on creativity,
honesty, and a shared vision for the future. UNISOGHN
is more than a group — it's a reflection of who we are,
where we've been, and where we're going.

Four voices. One vision. A story still unfolding.
