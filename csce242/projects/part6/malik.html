MALIK

Malik brings a unique perspective to UNISOGHN, blending raw
emotion with real life experiences. His sound is a mix of smooth
melodies and meaningful lyrics, creating music that not only
sounds good but also tells a story.

Growing up, music has always been his outlet — a way to express
thoughts, navigate challenges, and stay grounded. Whether in the
studio or on stage, Malik brings authenticity and an energy that
pushes the group forward.

As a key part of UNISOGHN, Malik continues to inspire through
his creativity, work ethic, and passion for making a lasting impact.
