JORDAN

Jordan's words give UNISOGHN its edge. As a vocalist
and writer, he brings depth, perspective, and real-life
experiences to the forefront. His lyrics are honest, raw,
and intentional.

Jordan believes in the power of storytelling, using his
voice to speak for the ones who feel unheard. His writing
reflects his growth, struggles, and dreams while pushing
the group's message forward.

Whether he's writing, recording, or performing, Jordan
creates with purpose and stays true to himself.
